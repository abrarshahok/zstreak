ZStreak ("we", "the app") is a habit-tracking app. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can have it deleted.

Data we collect

Account data (Firebase Authentication)

If you sign up or link an account, we collect your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your Google account's basic profile information (name, email, profile photo). This is used solely to authenticate you and keep your habit data tied to your account across devices. Guest ("Continue as guest") sessions do not collect any account data — your habits are stored only on your device until you choose to link an account.

Habit and check-in data

The habits you create and the days you mark complete are stored either locally on your device (guest mode, using local SQLite storage) or in our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore) if you have a linked account. This data is never shared with third parties and is used only to power the app's own features (your streaks, your grid, your reminders).

Purchase data (RevenueCat)

If you purchase a premium subscription, purchase and subscription-status data (such as which plan you bought and whether it is active) is processed by our payments provider, RevenueCat, and by Google Play Billing. We use this only to determine whether premium features should be unlocked for your account.

Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We collect anonymized usage data — such as which screens are opened, whether a habit was created, and whether a guest session was linked to an account — to understand how the app is used and to improve it. This data is not used to identify you personally beyond your app-internal user ID.

Crash and diagnostic data (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or encounters an error, technical diagnostic information (such as a stack trace and device model) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the problem. This data does not include your habit content.

Data deletion

If you have a linked account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This removes your Firestore data and your authentication record.

If you are using guest mode, your data lives only on your device. You can clear it at any time from Settings → Clear My Data, or simply uninstall the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, RevenueCat, Google Play Billing) as strictly necessary to provide the app's functionality, under their own privacy and data-processing terms.
